Quantum Well Explorer

A browser-based, install-free simulation of electron bound states in a GaAs / AlGaAs quantum well — the semiconductor heterostructure at the heart of lasers, detectors, and high-mobility transistors. Move a slider; the Schrödinger equation is solved live.

    How it maps to the pipeline

    Foundational: see that energy is quantised. Intermediate: change parameters, read and export the data. Advanced: the numerical method (Numerov integration with node-counting) is open in the source for students to read and modify.

    The model

    1-D time-independent Schrödinger equation in the effective-mass approximation (GaAs m* = 0.067 mₑ). Barrier height uses the common conduction-band-offset estimate ΔEₘ ≈ 0.748 x eV. Built to be read, not just run.

    Open & free by design

    No install, no account, no licensed software — runs on any browser, including a low-cost laptop in an under-resourced classroom. Released under the MIT License as a public, citable repository.
